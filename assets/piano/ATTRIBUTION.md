# Piano sample attribution

Piano samples: **Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm**, licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)**, distributed through the
[Tone.js audio repository](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander).
The instrument identified in the source README is a Yamaha C5.

The four MP3 files in this directory are copied unchanged from Tone.js's
Salamander collection. They are individual piano notes, not a recorded song.
The birthday site's musical arrangement uses these samples with playback-rate,
envelope, and reverb processing; the arrangement is separate from the sampled
instrument. No endorsement by the sample creator or Tone.js is implied.
