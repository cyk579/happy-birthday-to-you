# Background music

**Starlight — A Little Celebration** (`starlight-piano.mp3`)

An original 104 BPM, 64-bar arrangement, approximately 2 minutes 28 seconds long,
created for this birthday webpage. It combines a bright piano melody and
syncopated chords with a buoyant synthesized bass, light kick drum, handclaps
and shaker. The bass and percussion are generated by the rendering script;
no external drum samples are used. The melody does not quote the traditional
Happy Birthday song. The reproducible arrangement and renderer are in
[`scripts/render_music.py`](../scripts/render_music.py).

The local track is `assets/starlight-piano.mp3`. Its ending connects to the
opening for looped playback. Playback defaults to a low volume and fades out
when the microphone is requested or enabled, then returns when microphone use
ends or the candles are extinguished. Manual mute takes priority.

Piano recordings: **Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm**, Yamaha C5,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
obtained from the [Tone.js Salamander collection](https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/).
The source changelog also credits Markus Fiedler for retuning.

This track adapts the C2, C3, C4 and C5 samples through pitch shifting,
note envelopes, sequencing, stereo reflections and mixing with the synthesized
bass and percussion described above.
