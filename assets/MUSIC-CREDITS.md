# Background music

**Starlight — A Birthday Moment** (`starlight-piano.mp3`)

An original 56 BPM, 40-bar piano arrangement created for this birthday webpage,
with a soft synthesized pad and stereo reflections. The melody does not quote
the traditional Happy Birthday song. The reproducible arrangement and renderer
are in [`scripts/render_music.py`](../scripts/render_music.py).

Piano recordings: **Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm**, Yamaha C5,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
obtained from the [Tone.js Salamander collection](https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/).
The source changelog also credits Markus Fiedler for retuning.

This track adapts the C2, C3, C4 and C5 samples through pitch shifting,
note envelopes, sequencing, stereo reflections and mixing with an original pad.
No endorsement by Alexander Holm, Markus Fiedler, or Tone.js is implied.
Keep this credit with the MP3 when sharing or adapting the audio.

The unmodified source samples, pinned source URLs, checksums, original README
and complete license are in [`piano/ATTRIBUTION.md`](piano/ATTRIBUTION.md).
The piano-sample license does not relicense unrelated website code.
